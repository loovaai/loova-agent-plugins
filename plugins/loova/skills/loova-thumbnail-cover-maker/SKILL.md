# Thumbnail & Cover Maker

Design a cover that communicates at a small size. Build visual interest from the real content rather than invented events, exaggerated outcomes, or unsupported performance promises.

## Inputs and framing

Identify the topic, audience, placement, and locked copy. When footage is available, choose a credible visual anchor from its content. Use supplied person and brand references for identity. Do not rewrite approved text without permission.

For an otherwise unspecified video cover, 16:9 landscape or 9:16 vertical can be a working layout choice. Verify current destination templates or UI overlays when they matter; do not assume all platform covers share the same fixed dimensions.

## Workflow

1. Choose one visual proposition: a result, comparison, or question. Establish a reading order between subject, background, and text rather than combining unrelated hooks.
2. Use topic-relevant subjects and expressions. Reference covers may guide readability or color relationships without supplying unrelated people, branding, or exact copy.
3. Keep text concise unless the user requests longer copy. Preserve all locked words and adapt the layout rather than silently deleting them.
4. Separate the subject with light, edge contrast, whitespace, and appropriate text backing. Avoid decorative arrows, circles, and exaggerated expressions unless useful to the brief.
5. Read [Loova execution](references/execution.md). Use supported text rendering or composite exact text where available, then verify every word.

## Quality and delivery

Check landscape work around 320 pixels wide and vertical work around 180 pixels wide as readability tests, not platform export specifications. Confirm the subject and core words remain recognizable without zooming; check spelling and protected UI areas.

For requested alternatives, vary a meaningful concept or layout choice. Do not create unrequested paid variants. Repair readability through simpler backgrounds or text layout, and identity through reference constraints. Return the requested covers with actual dimensions and any unfinished text composition. Do not promise untested click-through or conversion gains.
