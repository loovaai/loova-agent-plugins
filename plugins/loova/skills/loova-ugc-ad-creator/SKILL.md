# UGC Ad Creator

Create a product ad with a coherent presenter, natural speech, and observable product value. Do not fabricate personal experience or testimonials to make the presentation sound authentic.

## Inputs and claims

Use product facts, audience, claims, presenter source, language, duration, and CTA. Preserve supplied scripts and presenter references. If no presenter is specified, a fictional adult creator can be proposed without implying a real customer endorsement.

Distinguish documented user experience, brand facts, and newly drafted ad copy. A statement such as "after using it for a month" needs supporting experience material; otherwise write an explanation or clearly framed demonstration. First-person language alone does not make a claim factual.

## Workflow

1. Select a main supported claim and visible demonstration. Write natural speech before assigning gestures. Reword, omit, or seek support for claims without evidence.
2. Break the actual script into speaking segments, product appearances, and supporting shots. Estimate only for planning; verify duration against generated speech rather than asserting lip sync from a reading-speed formula.
3. Separate presenter and product identity constraints. A product reference does not supply a person, and a person reference does not automatically supply clothing or background unless requested.
4. Read [Loova execution](references/execution.md). Verify a feasible route: native audiovisual generation, voice plus lip sync, or presenter shots with product inserts. Do not imply those capabilities exist just because image or video generation is connected.
5. Establish critical holding and contact poses before complex demonstrations. Prefer intelligible shots to unsupported hand changes, assembly steps, and transitions in one generation.
6. Assemble around actual speech length. If locked copy exceeds the requested duration, explain the conflict and offer a timing choice; do not silently delete words. Add captions when requested or included in the agreed delivery.

## Quality and delivery

Check hand anatomy, grip, orientation, and correspondence between gestures and speech. Confirm stable presenter appearance and voice, audible wording, complete endings, and actual lip sync. Ensure the visible demo supports the claims and that performance, sales, or testimonial statements are not invented.

Return a script for script-only requests or the assembled ad for production requests. State duration, audio, caption status, and incomplete assembly or lip-sync work. No media call is needed for a plan-only request.
